About

I work at the intersection of acoustics, computation, sustainability, and human perception — translating complex technical behaviour into systems people can actually use.

Computational Acoustics Decision-Support Tools Sustainable Materials Perceptual Acoustics Transfer Matrix Modelling Physics-Based Simulation AI-Assisted Workflows Python · MATLAB COMSOL Signal Processing AES Peer Reviewer

My PhD at Aalto Acoustics Lab focuses on foam-formed cellulose-based absorbers — reed fibres, lupine, softwood — measuring their acoustic properties, fitting physical models to data, and predicting real-room performance. I care about the full chain: from a specimen in an impedance tube to a specification an architect can use.

Before acoustics I trained as a nanoengineer at Universität Duisburg-Essen, which means I think about material behaviour across scales. Before that, I competed at the highest levels of breakdance, toured internationally as a theatre performer across Germany and Europe, and directed youth dance projects in the Ruhr. That background is not incidental — it shapes how I communicate, collaborate, and approach problems without clean answers.

I peer-review for the AES Journal. Open to Application Engineer and R&D roles from summer 2026.

Computational Tools & Applied Systems

Python · Browser · TMM

JCAL Two-Layer Absorption Calculator

Transfer-matrix calculator for layered porous absorbers, ported from Python to the browser. Live SVG cross-section, air gap slider, A/B orientation toggle. Built in parallel with the FA2026 paper.

Browser tool
Python · JCAL · Signal Processing

Reed & Lupine Analysis Pipeline

End-to-end Python pipeline for foam-formed bio-fibre characterisation: RokCell XML data loading, impedance tube processing, JCAL parameter fitting via optimisation, TMM prediction, and publication-ready figures.

Research code
Acoustics · Lumped Element · Browser

Dual-Neck Helmholtz Resonator Calculator

Frequency response calculator for MEMS microphone inlet design. Lumped-element modelling with end-correction logic. v1.4, fully browser-based.

Browser tool
ML · PyTorch · ONNX · Browser

Acoustic Material Identifier

Neural surrogate model for estimating JCAL transport parameters from impulse response measurements. PyTorch → ONNX → in-browser inference. JCAL forward model phase complete.

Artistic Work

A decade on stage.

Before acoustics research became my primary work, I spent more than ten years as a professional dancer, performer, and choreographer — competing at world level, touring internationally, and making work with some of Germany's leading theatre houses. That chapter is complete. What it left behind is a way of thinking about bodies, space, and sound that doesn't fit neatly into an engineering CV.

I performed in breakdance and contemporary dance productions across Germany and Europe, working with Samir Akika, Neco Celik, Julio Cesar Iglesias Ungo, Susanne Linke, and Lin Verleger. Venues included Goethe Theater Bremen, Schauspielhaus Bochum, Tanzhaus NRW, and international festivals.

Alongside performance, I created sound design for live theatre and directed youth dance projects as artistic director — including at Flottmannhallen Herne and Grillo Theater Essen.
